Virtual Truck Race

Finish: 900 m

How to play

  • Press Start Race to send three trucks down the highway.
  • Control the orange truck with the arrow keys: ↑ accelerate, ↓ brake, ←/→ switch lanes.
  • Maintain speed, avoid drifting off the lane, and beat the AI trucks to the finish line.
  • The first truck to reach the finish distance wins. Reset anytime to try again.
